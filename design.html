Simple Workflow

How The Lost & Found System Works

Designed to safely verify ownership and return lost items to their rightful owners at ACT.

01

Search or Report

Browse active listings or instantly report a lost/found item with descriptions, location, and photos.

02

Verification & Claim Request

Click "Claim Item" and fill out the verification details (unique marks, serial numbers, wallpaper description).

03

Reclaim at Security Office

Present your official ACT Student / Employee ID at the Campus Security or Prefect Office to collect your item.

Claiming Policy & Requirements

To prevent unauthorized claims and safeguard student property, please observe the following requirements upon retrieval:

  • Must present a valid ACT Student / Faculty ID Card or official registration form.
  • For electronics (phones, laptops), you must be able to unlock the device or describe lockscreen wallpapers.
  • Unclaimed items after 60 days will be handed to the Prefect of Discipline for administrative disposition.
  • Claim requests submitted online will receive an automated confirmation email with a verification code.
